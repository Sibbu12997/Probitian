# ProBitian Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As contributors and maintainers of the **ProBitian** ecosystem, we are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free learning and collaboration environment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

ProBitian exists to empower aspiring and professional Data Analysts, BI Developers, and Analytics Engineers. We welcome learners, developers, and educators of all skill levels to learn, contribute, and grow together.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive and productive environment include:

- **Welcoming Learners**: Fostering an encouraging environment where beginners feel comfortable asking questions about Power BI, SQL, DAX, Python, Excel, or data architectures.
- **Respectful & Constructive Communication**: Using welcoming and inclusive language, offering constructive criticism with actionable advice, and focusing on the technical merits of code and educational content.
- **Graceful Acceptance of Feedback**: Being open to feedback on pull requests, documentation clarity, and architectural designs.
- **Focus on the Community**: Prioritizing the educational outcomes of students, data practitioners, and open-source contributors.
- **Intellectual Integrity**: Respecting original authorship, properly attributing external datasets or third-party resources, and adhering to copyright guidelines.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, stalking, or doxxing.
- Publishing others' private information (such as physical or email addresses) without explicit permission.
- Submitting spam, promotional links, or low-effort AI-generated content without technical review and validation.
- Submitting malicious code, exploits, or intentionally weakened security controls.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source learning environment.

---

## Scope & Responsibilities

This Code of Conduct applies within all official ProBitian project spaces, including the GitHub repository, pull request discussions, issue trackers, Community Hub interactions, and official communication channels. It also applies when an individual is officially representing ProBitian in public spaces.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

---

## Enforcement & Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the ProBitian project maintainer:

- **Project Owner**: Shivam Singh
- **Official Email**: [probitianofficial@gmail.com](mailto:probitianofficial@gmail.com)

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly, confidentially, and fairly. The maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

### Enforcement Guidelines

1. **Correction**: A private, written warning providing clarity on the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning**: A formal warning with consequences for continued unacceptable behavior.
3. **Temporary Suspension**: A temporary ban from participating in project repositories, issues, and discussions.
4. **Permanent Ban**: A permanent ban from any public interaction within the ProBitian project and community for severe or repeated violations.
